Writing robot characters is hard in that it requires kind of a bottom up re-centering of yourself every time.

I guess it's the same process that actors do when they get into character.
Problem with writing is that you have to jump back and forth, over and over. When you give a character their own chapter it's easier, it allows you to keep a grip of the character and their way of thinking.
When you jump back and forth there is a high risk of slipping up, usually it's not a huge misstake but you end up making tonal miss steps that will take some of your audience out of the book


Sparks of Hope is a worse novel, i am sure if someone with a degree in litterature readis it they can make a mound of all the nits they pick from the last chapter. but the story is good. I ran out of story before i reached the ending, which leads to a lot of time skips in the ending.
It goes to fast, it needs more time to breathe.

But I had gone on the journey with the characters, I was done.
